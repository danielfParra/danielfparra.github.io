## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment:** Does NOT read as obviously AI-made — it has real personality (the "Plate 01 / On view / MMXXVI" photographic-archive framing, the mint accent sourced from the subject's glasses, the expandable-paper interaction, a real portrait, the keyword marquee). But it sits squarely in the *editorial-typographic* reflex-reject lane (serif display + mono labels + ruled separators + monochrome dark), and the single clearest tell is the **numbered section eyebrow on every section** (§ 02 Approach, § 03 Research, § 04 CV, § 05 Contact). That cadence is AI section-grammar; it's the one thing making an otherwise-distinctive page legible as templated.

**Deterministic scan (detect.mjs, 7 findings):**
- `numbered-section-markers` (advisory) — sequence 01–06; agrees with the LLM's top tell.
- `em-dash-overuse` (warning) — 8 in body text; these are the verbatim paper abstracts plus 2 decorative `<h4>` markers. Site-voice em-dashes were already removed; abstracts left intentionally (reproduced scholarly text).
- `overused-font` ×2 (warning) — Inter. A committed identity choice (paired with Google Sans Flex display + Geist Mono); identity-preservation applies, but noted.
- `layout-transition` ×2 (warning) — `transition: padding` on nav shrink (line 55) and `transition: padding-left` on `.cv-item` hover (line 220). Real perf nits the LLM under-weighted.

**Visual overlays:** No live overlay injected. Browser evidence is three real rendered screenshots (hero, research focus state, paper-open) captured via Chrome DevTools Protocol during verification.

## Overall Impression
A genuinely well-crafted, cohesive page that clears the AI-slop bar on personality and consistency. The biggest gap is strategic, not cosmetic: PRODUCT.md asks for **warm, approachable, human**, and the execution reads cool, austere, and clinical. The second gap is a broken mobile navigation. The single biggest opportunity: resolve the warm/austere tension so the feeling matches the stated brand, while trimming the numbered-eyebrow scaffolding that flattens the voice.

## What's Working
- **A real, consistent design system.** Tokens, the serif/mono/Inter trio, hairline rules, tonal layering — applied with discipline across every section. Scores a true 4 on consistency.
- **The expandable research list is the right affordance.** It turns a publication dump into curated, scannable specimens; each row gives title + one-line finding, expands to the abstract and links. This is the page's thesis as a component.
- **Committed, sourced accent.** The mint is rationed and meaningful (active nav, findings, the open-paper scale-bar), not decorative confetti.

## Priority Issues

- **[P1] Mobile navigation disappears with no replacement.** Below 960px `nav.top ul { display:none }` and there is no hamburger/menu. On a phone the only nav is the brand link and the CTA; a visitor can't jump to Research/CV/Contact and must scroll the entire long page. Hurts the journalist-on-mobile and student-on-mobile audiences named in PRODUCT.md.
  - **Fix:** Add a mobile menu (disclosure button toggling the existing anchor list, or a compact bottom/section jump bar). Keep it keyboard-operable.
  - **Suggested command:** /impeccable adapt

- **[P2] Warm/human goal unmet (tonal mismatch).** PRODUCT.md brand personality is "warm · precise · inviting"; the rendered page is precise and cool but not warm or inviting. Warmth currently lives only in copy, not in the visual treatment.
  - **Fix:** Carry warmth through the levers PRODUCT.md names — a touch more warmth in the ink/surface temperature, more generous type warmth, imagery, and softer affordances — without abandoning the dark identity.
  - **Suggested command:** /impeccable bolder (toward warmth) or /impeccable colorize

- **[P2] Numbered section eyebrow on every section reads as AI scaffolding.** § 02 / § 03 / § 04 / § 05 above each heading is the editorial-AI cadence (confirmed by the detector). The sections aren't an ordered sequence the reader needs to count.
  - **Fix:** Drop the numbers or replace the uniform tracked-mono eyebrow with a cadence that varies per section; keep at most one deliberate numbered sequence (the papers already number themselves).
  - **Suggested command:** /impeccable typeset

- **[P2] External links open in the same tab with no `target`/`rel`.** Every paper, PDF, and social link navigates away; on a research site whose whole purpose is sending people to papers, the visitor loses their place and rarely returns.
  - **Fix:** Add `target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"` to off-site links (papers, PDFs, infographic, social). Keep in-page anchors same-tab.
  - **Suggested command:** /impeccable harden

- **[P3] Layout-property transitions.** Nav shrink animates `padding`; `.cv-item` hover animates `padding-left`. Both trigger layout. Swap to `transform` (e.g. `translateX` for the cv hover) or animate a fixed height.
  - **Suggested command:** /impeccable optimize

## Persona Red Flags

**Casey (Distracted Mobile User):** The hidden nav is the headline failure — no way to reach a section without scrolling the full page one-handed. The "Get in touch" CTA is top-right (out of the thumb zone). Paper toggle target is 36px (<44px), though the whole row is tappable so it's usable.

**Sam (Accessibility-Dependent):** Much improved this session — papers are keyboard-operable with `aria-expanded`, focus rings are visible, labels now clear ~4.5:1. Remaining: no skip-to-content link; mobile menu gap also affects keyboard users on small screens; verify the mint year text isn't the *only* signal of recency (it's paired with the number, so OK).

**The Journalist (project persona — press, on deadline, often mobile):** Lands wanting the plain-language takeaway. The one-line hooks deliver it well. But on mobile they can't jump to a specific paper, and clicking "Psychology Today" / a PDF navigates them away from the site with no easy return.

## Minor Observations
- `<title>` and two decorative `<h4>` markers still use em dashes (meta/decorative, low impact; the h4 dashes inflate the detector's body-copy count).
- No Esc-to-close on an open paper (minor control gap).
- Portrait alt text is bare ("Daniel F. Parra"); brand voice could make it richer.
- Display heading is 136px (above impeccable's ~96px ceiling) at -0.05em tracking (below the -0.04em floor) — deliberate brand exceptions, documented in DESIGN.md.

## Questions to Consider
- Does the cool, austere execution serve the "warm, inviting" brand goal, or fight it?
- Should section navigation survive on mobile, or is full-page scroll acceptable for these audiences?
- Do the §-numbered section eyebrows earn their place, or are they scaffolding the page would be stronger without?
